FAQ

Q. The calculation result seems wrong.
Please check the angle mode (DEG / RAD / GRAD) in the Settings (⚙) menu.

Q. My history is gone.
History is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app also removes all history.

Q. Does it work on iPad?
Yes. Calculator Pro is fully optimized for all iPhone and iPad screen sizes.
