Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Calculator Pro does not collect any personal information. All calculation history is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted externally.

2. Third-Party Services

For certain advanced mathematical expressions, the app may send the expression string to an external computation server (SymPy). No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

3. Data Storage

All calculation history is stored only in your device's local storage. This data is permanently deleted when the app is uninstalled.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
